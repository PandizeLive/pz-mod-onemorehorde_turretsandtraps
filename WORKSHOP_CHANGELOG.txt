v0.2.4
Single-player development test content
- Added persistent test defenses with readable white developer markers and direction arrows.
- Added 1x2 Spike Barriers with shared durability, rotation and removal; front and side attacks cause 10 damage by default, while the rear is safe. Damage and maximum durability are configurable in Sandbox.
- Added player vaulting through the Spike Barrier submenu for newly built/deployed barriers: fence-style collision, safe rear crossing and a 20 percent front-side scratch risk. Older built barriers can be picked up/redeployed to convert.
- Added single-player Spike Barrier construction under Carpentry and Turrets & Traps, with a rotating two-tile preview: Carpentry 1, four interchangeable planks/wooden spears, four binding units and a retained sharp knife/cleaver; awards Carpentry XP. Sandbox master/barrier switches preserve placed structures when disabled.
- Added craftable portable Spike Barriers using the same basic recipe; deploy one finished item through Building without a second material charge or XP award. Native tile identities preserve placed barrier parts after saving.
- Added Turrets & Traps to Crafting, readable barrier statistics and a world condition display. Pick up or freely repair a basic barrier in ten seconds; pickup restores full durability. Bundle five barriers with 67% reduced encumbrance, with no packing/unpacking XP.
- Added manually fired potato turrets with a one-second cadence, calibrated damage, animated knockdowns and tiny impact splash.
- Added optional Pixel Indicator damage feedback and developer diagnostics.
Local tools
- Added the OMH release menu with verified local installation and debug-log capture.

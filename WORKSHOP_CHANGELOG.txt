v0.2.23
Single-player development test content
- Added persistent test defenses with readable white developer markers and direction arrows.
- Added 1x2 Spike Barriers with shared durability and removal; see-through connected runs have front collision and outer end caps, with an open rear. Newly exposed ends are capped after removal; front and side attacks cause 10 damage by default, while the rear is safe. Damage and maximum durability are configurable in Sandbox.
- Added player vaulting with native Interact/run/sprint controls and low-fence reactions. Spike-side scratches occur at crossing contact with configurable 1-in-N risk; rear and end-cap crossings avoid spike scratches. Older full-square barriers can be picked up/redeployed to convert.
- Added connected perpendicular corner placement without internal end caps, collision-line previews and an optional collision overlay aligned through the UI render pass. Sandbox pages use OMH: T&T prefixes, ordered General, Traps, Turrets, Automation, Modules, Loot and Debug, with defense headings inside the parent pages.
- Added single-player Spike Barrier construction under OMH: Traps, with a rotating two-tile preview: Carpentry 1, four interchangeable planks/wooden spears, four binding units and a retained sharp knife/cleaver; awards Carpentry XP. Sandbox master/barrier switches preserve placed structures when disabled.
- Added craftable portable Spike Barriers using the same basic recipe; deploy one finished item through Building without a second material charge or XP award. Native tile identities preserve placed barrier parts after saving.
- Added OMH: Traps and OMH: Turrets categories to Crafting/Building, readable barrier statistics and a world condition display. Right-click selection covers either half and shared corners, with whole-barrier hover highlighting. Pick up or repair either barrier tier without materials in ten seconds, using reachable approaches with stall cancellation; pickup restores full durability. Loose basic/reinforced barriers weigh 4/5; five-item same-tier packs weigh 6.6/8.25, retaining 67% reduced encumbrance and no packing/unpacking XP.
- Added reinforced Spike Barriers: upgrade with Carpentry 3, two planks, eight nails and a retained hammer for 30 base XP. Upgrading also restores full durability. Defaults to 25 damage and twice basic durability, configurable in Sandbox. Reinforced items retain their tier through pickup, deployment and separate bundles.
- Destroyed turret bases drop two scrap metal and preserve their actual stored head, ammo, modules, expansion kit and battery without changing condition or charge. Loose technical placeholder sprites use a smaller world size.
- Added craftable modular turret bases and potato heads, with pump/improvised recipes and native deployment. Inventory Install reuses native rotating placement for the exact selected packed turret, handles preselected inputs and reports blocked attempts. Loaded pickup preserves actual ammunition, components, battery charge, condition and cooldowns; carried weight includes contents and a tooltip lists supplies. Reinforced barriers use independent texture inputs.
- Added manually fired potato turrets consuming real potatoes and battery/grid/generator power, with 100-potato default storage, a one-round magazine, overlapping two-second reload / cooldown and a two-second sustained default cadence, 35 direct-hit damage, animated knockdowns and 25-percent impact splash within 1.5 tiles, capped at three secondary targets. The Turrets Sandbox page controls durability, ammo/module storage, combat and power costs. Detection and Firing modules enable automatic defense, Filter selects target priority, including initial-health Max Toughness; unfiltered random and Max Toughness targets remain selected until invalid, and 360 expands the placement-locked forward cone. Rapid-Fire, Reload, Velocity and Range modules stack with adjustable limits; Autoloader eliminates reload delay. Small, Medium and Large Expansion Kits use one dedicated socket, with configurable ammo/slot bonuses, safe removal and preserved contents. Manage shows effective Reload and Fire rate.
- Added a single-player firearm-head prototype supporting the M9 pistol, JS-2000 shotgun and MSR788 rifle on the universal base. Actual guns retain attachments, condition and loaded ammunition; matching loose ammo, powered feeding, pump/bolt cycles, shared modules, native gun models/sounds and grouped firearm tuning are supported. Pistol requires an inserted magazine; Manage accepts a matching carried magazine and supports timed ejection while retaining chambered rounds. Missing requirements are highlighted, and heads can be swapped while reserve stays in the base. Mounted guns use an upright pose and a white technical support; native firearm tracers use the correct rendering interface. Accuracy and shotgun spread use explicit turret approximations. Native gun ranges and critical chance/damage are retained, with stackable Accuracy/Critical modules and tunable gains. Initial pistol chambering does not slow its repeated shots.
- Added loot-only Firing, Detection, Filter, 360, Rapid-Fire, Reload, Velocity, Range, Autoloader, Suppression, Critical and Accuracy modules, plus three Expansion Kit tiers, in electronics storage and themed electrician, gun-accessory and mechanic tool pools. Grouped Sandbox controls adjust automation, cone width, detection interval, module gains/caps and overall/per-module loot abundance.
- Added optional Pixel Indicator damage feedback and developer diagnostics.
- Added stationary base tiles and independent cube-placeholder turret heads with a sprite fallback, see-through 1x1 base collision, adjacent pickup, and a dedicated Manage window with head/ammo/module/battery slots, timed drag/drop, live supplies/stats, manual Fire, Power and Priority. Ten compact module slots show locked capacity; installed names are shortened, extra Sandbox rows scroll, and changed transfer results briefly brighten. Invalid transfers explain why; packed-turret tooltips show charge without a formatting error; the base and sprite fallback share a grounded anchor, with lowered model heads and matching aim. Placement and selection show effective coverage and independent aim direction, with selection display toggleable in General settings. Manage opens independently of player inventories and uses one persistent Power switch for firing, tracking and feeding. Powered detectors sweep their heads while searching within fixed coverage; Sandbox controls the visual sweep speed. Potato shots show a brown projectile starting at the barrel tip and a centered brown splat, with configurable travel speed delaying damage until arrival. Owner-supplied thump and splat variants use subtle pitch variation. Potato reload and firearm loading/racking have audio feedback. Suppression reduces zombie hearing by 60% for one copy and 75% for two at defaults, with diminishing slot-limited stacking that never reaches silence and selects balanced muffled firing sounds; hearing strength, potato noise radius and loot abundance are configurable; unknown firing events retain their original sound with adjustable fallback volume reduction. Mounted guns bypass the static item-image cache while turning. Component IDs match their names with one definition per component. Heads turn at a Sandbox-adjustable speed and wait for alignment before firing, including a single queued manual shot with bounded target searches across ticks; known automatic targets avoid redundant scans. Hybrid shots avoid repeated inventory scans and unchanged configuration refreshes; detailed shot logs drain in bounded batches with timing diagnostics. The placeholder base has matching cube depth for its raised shape. Turret previews no longer show barrier outlines; turret recipes retain screwdriver plus wrench/pipe wrench.
- Added the owner-supplied Turrets & Traps mod-list icon.

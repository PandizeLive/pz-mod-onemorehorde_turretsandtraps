Single-player development test content
- Added loot-only Piercing, Initial Blow, Execute and Impact Modules, with grouped Sandbox controls and matching LootLab entries. Piercing extends single-projectile firearm hits through aligned zombies with reduced damage; Initial Blow boosts hits on healthy zombies; Execute finishes wounded targets; Impact can stagger a standing target on the first shot after an empty reload. Module effects preserve first-shot entitlement on the exact head and use bounded shared secondary processing.
- Unified firing power: each working module adds 5 percent of base cost, with stronger and longer-range weapons consuming more per volley. Battery and generator defaults are 0.005 percent charge / 0.0001 fuel units before multipliers, with no turret battery idle drain. Manage displays actual firing cost. Replaced separate Splitter/Fragment surcharges; existing saved base-cost settings remain unchanged.
- Added loot-only Enhanced Firing and Enhanced Detection, replacing their normal roles. Enhanced Firing uses full native-derived firearm cadence while retaining Rapid-Fire; Enhanced Detection turns three times faster and scans at 0.10-second intervals. Both match Large Expansion loot rarity. Automatic-capable turret heads use their Auto capability without altering handheld fire modes. Ready acquired targets keep firing during background scans.
- Removed the diagnostic registry cap from real Spike Barrier placement, extension and rotation. Splitter and Fragment tracers display together and clip at visible boundaries. Spawn item IDs use consistent OMHTTTrap, OMHTTTurret and OMHTTModule prefixes; fresh test worlds are required.
- Added lootable Magazine, Splitter and Fragment Modules with grouped Sandbox tuning. Magazine gives linear 35-percent capacity growth with at least one extra round per copy; Splitter adds half-damage side shots and spends available ready rounds; Fragment creates five direct radial fragments on the first primary hit. Extra firearm rounds remain real, weight-bearing items and return to reserve when capacity or the head is removed. Secondary effects use a bounded shared queue.
- Added one-tile backed Barbed Panel for walls, fences, ordinary single-panel doors and window frames. Zombies take 10 damage immediately on contact and every two seconds while touching; adjacent sections share the interval. Door barbs follow the leaf; window barbs stay across open or smashed glass with separate 50 durability. Supports without persistent damageable HP also use 50 barb durability, losing one per damage activation; other supports share native health. Weighs two; inventory attachment respects Build Cheat and explains missing tools. Includes crafting, inventory Attach Trap, ten-second repair and five-second removal, grouped Traps settings and two technical texture states with four-direction placement. No added support HP or collision; multi-panel and garage doors are not yet supported.
- Added basic and reinforced 1x2 Bed Spikes: 40-percent zombie trip chance with a native forward collapse, recovery and trip/landing/impact sounds, 20/50 damage on fresh falls, six durability wear on the hit tile per fall, and five seconds of trip protection after getting up. Existing prone zombies take no repeated damage. Each tile has independent 50/100 durability and weighs 1.5/2. Crafting produces two items; place them together or recover/redeploy a single tile. Repair, reinforcement, pickup and breakage affect only the selected tile. Walking and running use bush-like brush-through animations; walking is safe, running/sprinting risks scratches and sprinting has a configurable 20-percent fall chance. Actual player falls cause an environmental laceration once; noclip/ghost bypass traversal effects. Includes crafting, reinforcement, ten-second free repair and five-second pickup, clear-floor placement beside backed panels, and tuning under Traps. One repeated white technical tile covers the bed.

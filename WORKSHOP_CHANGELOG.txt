Single-player development test content
- Added basic and reinforced 1x2 Bed Spikes: chance-based zombie trips, 20/50 damage on fresh falls, six shared durability wear per fall, and five seconds of trip protection after getting up. Existing prone zombies take no repeated damage. Walking players brush through safely; running/sprinting risks scratches. Includes construction, reinforcement, ten-second free repair/pickup, portable redeployment and Sandbox tuning, with temporary white technical markers.
- Reduced unnecessary turret reaction tracking when detailed diagnostics are off and added separate audio-stage timings to investigate reported pauses. The client audio stall remains under investigation.
- Added independent OMH: Traps and OMH: Turrets mod entries: enable either or both, with component-specific settings, enable switches and diagnostics. This development package split uses fresh worlds.
- Added persistent test defenses with readable white developer markers and direction arrows.
- Added 1x2 Spike Barriers with shared durability and removal; see-through connected runs have front collision and outer end caps, with an open rear. Newly exposed ends are capped after removal; front and side attacks cause 10 damage by default, while the rear is safe. Damage and maximum durability are configurable in Sandbox.
- Added player vaulting with native Interact/run/sprint controls and low-fence reactions. Spike-side scratches occur at crossing contact with configurable 1-in-N risk; rear and end-cap crossings avoid spike scratches. Older full-square barriers can be picked up/redeployed to convert.
- Added connected perpendicular corner placement without internal end caps, collision-line previews and an optional collision overlay aligned through the UI render pass. Sandbox pages use OMH: T&T prefixes, ordered General, Traps, Turrets, Automation, Modules, Loot and Debug, with defense headings inside the parent pages.
- Added single-player Spike Barrier construction under OMH: Traps, with a rotating two-tile preview: Carpentry 1, four interchangeable planks/wooden spears, four binding units and a retained sharp knife/cleaver; awards Carpentry XP. Sandbox master/barrier switches preserve placed structures when disabled.
- Added craftable portable Spike Barriers using the same basic recipe; deploy one finished item through Building without a second material charge or XP award. Native tile identities preserve placed barrier parts after saving.
- Added OMH: Traps and OMH: Turrets categories to Crafting/Building, readable barrier statistics and a world condition display. Right-click selection covers either half and shared corners, with whole-barrier hover highlighting. Pick up or repair either barrier tier without materials in ten seconds, using reachable approaches with stall cancellation; pickup restores full durability. Loose basic/reinforced barriers weigh 4/5; five-item same-tier packs weigh 6.6/8.25, retaining 67% reduced encumbrance and no packing/unpacking XP.
- Added reinforced Spike Barriers: upgrade with Carpentry 3, two planks, eight nails and a retained hammer for 30 base XP. Upgrading also restores full durability. Defaults to 25 damage and twice basic durability, configurable in Sandbox. Reinforced items retain their tier through pickup, deployment and separate bundles.
- Destroyed turret bases drop two scrap metal and preserve their actual stored head, ammo, modules, expansion kit and battery without changing condition or charge. Loose technical placeholder sprites use a smaller world size.
- Added craftable modular turret bases and potato heads, with pump/improvised recipes and native deployment. Inventory Install reuses native rotating placement for the exact selected packed turret, handles preselected inputs and reports blocked attempts. Loaded pickup preserves actual ammunition, components, battery charge, condition and cooldowns; carried weight includes contents and a tooltip lists supplies. Reinforced barriers use independent texture inputs.
- Added manually fired potato turrets consuming real potatoes and battery/grid/generator power, with 100-potato default storage, a one-round magazine, overlapping two-second reload / cooldown and a two-second sustained default cadence, 35 direct-hit damage, animated knockdowns and 25-percent impact splash within 1.5 tiles, capped at three secondary targets. The Turrets Sandbox page controls durability, ammo/module storage, combat and power costs. Detection and Firing modules enable automatic defense, Filter selects target priority, including initial-health Max Toughness; unfiltered random and Max Toughness targets remain selected until invalid, and 360 expands the placement-locked forward cone. Rapid-Fire, Reload, Velocity and Range modules stack with adjustable limits; Autoloader eliminates reload delay. Small, Medium and Large Expansion Kits use one dedicated socket, with configurable ammo/slot bonuses, safe removal and preserved contents. Manage shows effective Reload and Fire rate, plus a persistent Engagement range slider with Max.
- Added single-player conventional firearm heads on the universal base, using native gun data for all twenty real vanilla guns and compatible conventional modded weapons; unsupported custom mechanisms are rejected. Actual guns retain attachments, condition and loaded ammunition; matching loose ammo, powered feeding, pump/bolt cycles, shared modules, native gun models/sounds and grouped firearm tuning are supported. Pistol requires an inserted magazine; Manage accepts a matching carried magazine and supports timed ejection while retaining chambered rounds. Missing requirements are highlighted, and heads can be swapped while reserve stays in the base. Mounted guns use an upright pose and a white technical support; native firearm tracers use the correct rendering interface. Accuracy improves at closer distances, with configurable maximum-range defaults of 60 percent for ordinary guns and 80 percent for identified precision/semi-auto rifles; shotgun spread remains a turret approximation. Native gun ranges and critical chance/damage are retained, with stackable Accuracy/Critical modules and tunable gains. Initial pistol chambering does not slow its repeated shots. Automatic firing uses native-derived cycle timing with adjustable global firearm rate, defaulting to 0.75. Acquired targets fire independently of scan intervals.
- Added loot-only Firing, Detection, Filter, 360, Rapid-Fire, Reload, Velocity, Range, Autoloader, Suppression, Critical and Accuracy modules, plus three Expansion Kit tiers, in electronics, electrician, gun-accessory, mechanic, garage and tool-store pools. Lower ordinary pool weights favor one/two-component finds while richer pools retain uncommon five-plus jackpots. Grouped Sandbox controls adjust automation, cone width, detection interval, module gains/caps and overall/per-module loot abundance. Approved 5x loot abundance is baked into base weights, with overall/per-component controls at 1. Additional mechanic, computer and radio pools broaden ordinary finds.

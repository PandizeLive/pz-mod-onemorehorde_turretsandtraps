v0.2.9
Single-player development test content
- Added persistent test defenses with readable white developer markers and direction arrows.
- Added 1x2 Spike Barriers with shared durability and removal; see-through connected runs have front collision and outer end caps, with an open rear. Newly exposed ends are capped after removal; front and side attacks cause 10 damage by default, while the rear is safe. Damage and maximum durability are configurable in Sandbox.
- Added player vaulting with native Interact/run/sprint controls and low-fence reactions. Spike-side scratches occur at crossing contact with configurable 1-in-N risk; rear and end-cap crossings avoid spike scratches. Older full-square barriers can be picked up/redeployed to convert.
- Added connected perpendicular corner placement without internal end caps, collision-line previews and an optional collision overlay aligned through the UI render pass. Sandbox controls are grouped by defense, General and Debug.
- Added single-player Spike Barrier construction under Carpentry and Turrets & Traps, with a rotating two-tile preview: Carpentry 1, four interchangeable planks/wooden spears, four binding units and a retained sharp knife/cleaver; awards Carpentry XP. Sandbox master/barrier switches preserve placed structures when disabled.
- Added craftable portable Spike Barriers using the same basic recipe; deploy one finished item through Building without a second material charge or XP award. Native tile identities preserve placed barrier parts after saving.
- Added Turrets & Traps to Crafting, readable barrier statistics and a world condition display. Right-click selection covers either half and shared corners, with whole-barrier hover highlighting. Pick up or repair either barrier tier without materials in ten seconds, using reachable approaches with stall cancellation; pickup restores full durability. Loose basic/reinforced barriers weigh 4/5; five-item same-tier packs weigh 6.6/8.25, retaining 67% reduced encumbrance and no packing/unpacking XP.
- Added reinforced Spike Barriers: upgrade with Carpentry 3, two planks, eight nails and a retained hammer for 30 base XP. Upgrading also restores full durability. Defaults to 25 damage and twice basic durability, configurable in Sandbox. Reinforced items retain their tier through pickup, deployment and separate bundles.
- Added craftable modular turret bases and potato heads, with pump/improvised recipes and native deployment. Loaded pickup preserves actual ammunition, components, battery charge, condition and cooldowns; carried weight includes contents and a tooltip lists supplies. Reinforced barriers use independent texture inputs.
- Added manually fired potato turrets consuming real potatoes and battery/grid/generator power, with a one-second default cadence, calibrated damage, animated knockdowns and tiny impact splash. A dedicated Sandbox page controls durability, ammo/module storage, combat and power costs. Module storage is available for development testing; automatic targeting and module bonuses remain in development.
- Added optional Pixel Indicator damage feedback and developer diagnostics.
- Added the owner-supplied Turrets & Traps mod-list icon.
Local tools
- Added the OMH release menu with verified local installation and debug-log capture.

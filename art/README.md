## Owner art policy and technical requirements

The owner will source final art without AI. Do not generate concept art, stylistic designs, textures, icons or final visual assets, or choose aesthetic direction. The explicitly requested unstyled white cube-only mechanical placeholder is the exception. Its proportions and appearance must not become requirements for the final artist.

Limit the development brief to functional needs: readable facing/state, required pivots/attachments, placement footprint and supported animation states. The final artist decides form, style, materials and detailing with the owner. Keep all placeholder outputs separate from production artwork.

Keep editable originals separate from exported game assets when production begins. Create source/, textures/, models/ and audio/ only as needed. Final sprite dimensions, rotations, anchors, collision properties and export conventions require the current-game asset prototype.

## Asset provenance

For every introduced asset, record filename, author/source, creation method, permission/license, editable original and export destination. Production art is owner-sourced without AI. The procedural white cube placeholder is identified explicitly as a technical test asset. Researching another mod's behavior does not authorize copying its assets.

P1C1 adds `OMHTT_TestCube.png` under the package's `42.20/media/textures/`. It is a static 128x256 transparent render of the existing approved cube GLB, generated by [build_foundation_sprite.py](../tools/build_foundation_sprite.py) from that original geometry. No external art, image generation, decoration or textures are used. Neutral grayscale shading makes the white cube faces readable. This is a temporary lifecycle marker, not a native 3D import or new art-direction proposal.

OMH branding templates may be reused only after checking the actual current workspace assets and intended usage. Do not invent a public download link from a local file or publish concept art as implemented gameplay.

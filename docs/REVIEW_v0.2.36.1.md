## Known boundaries, not newly discovered regressions

- Combat remains SP-only. Explicit client/server gates prevent calling this multiplayer-ready; a networked combat conversion belongs to R1C3/authority work, not this menu patch.
- Barbed Panel does not injure players; multi-panel/garage doors are rejected. Windows have independent 50-HP barbs, ordinary finite supports share support health, and other eligible supports use independent durability. Those are documented scope/policy differences, not accidental health inflation.
- Bed fall damage is once per fresh landing, not continuous damage to already-prone zombies/crawlers. Repeated falls remain possible after recovery protection. This differs intentionally from timed barb contact.
- Native list view supports these collapsible groups; icon view stays flat. A custom icon hierarchy would add UI maintenance and compatibility cost. Recommend keeping native behavior for now.
- The reviewed module-loot injector adds/removes recognized OMHTT component entries in 16 named pools; it does not change global rolls or vanilla item weights. This does not establish the cause of earlier well-stocked medical containers or rule out another mod/settings interaction.
- Latest captured owner console remains the 0.2.36 snapshot documented in TEST_v0.2.36.md: five native missing-groundcover ERROR lines after startup, no Exception/STACK TRACE matches. No new owner test or fresh error-free console is claimed.

## Recommended order

Complete the short rendered check, then fix misleading automatic-range presentation and recipe descriptions/naming together. Profile the existing dense-client pauses before adding more combat content. Add bounded barb contact audio as a separate approved polish change. Final art and multiplayer remain their own checkpoints; this review does not reorder or approve the roadmap.

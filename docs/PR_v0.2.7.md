# See-through barriers and reinforced upgrades

Barriers now permit sight through their fronts and exposed ends while retaining native collision and corner thump ownership. Continued running beyond the vault landing tile no longer makes a completed crossing appear interrupted in diagnostics; native-entry timeouts receive additional state context.

Reinforce an existing barrier with Carpentry 3, two planks, eight nails and a retained hammer for 30 base XP. Defaults are 25 damage and twice basic durability, with useful controls under Spike Barrier. Upgrading preserves existing damage. Free ten-second pickup/Repair and full-condition recovery apply to both tiers; reinforced items retain their tier through native deployment and separate five-item bundles with the same 67 percent weight reduction.
